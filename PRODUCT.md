# Nang Dalet Portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Employers and prospective clients are equally important audiences. Visitors evaluate Nang Dalet's software development work and can download a CV or make contact.

## Product Purpose

Present software development capabilities and project evidence to support employment opportunities and client conversations.

## Operating Context

The existing Next.js site combines a scroll-driven Three.js project journey with conventional about, skills, projects, and contact sections. Visitors can navigate directly to those sections.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve access to the eight existing projects, CV download, and contact methods during visual improvements.
- The contact form uses a server-side Telegram integration; credentials must remain server-only.
- Current request: improve the existing 3D experience through code.
- Retain mobile navigation, reduced-motion behavior, pause controls, and static alternatives when WebGL is unavailable.
- Career details, availability, project descriptions, and contact information are existing repository content; their current accuracy has not been confirmed by the owner. Do not invent or update factual claims without confirmation.

## Brand Commitments

The portfolio represents Nang Dalet, a software developer. Existing content emphasizes backend APIs and business applications.

## Evidence on Hand

- Eight project records and screenshot paths in `lib/projects.ts`.
- Daily Expense Tracker: owner-supplied live URL and dashboard screenshot at `public/DailyExpenseTracker.png`; feature descriptions are based on the supplied screenshots, with no framework or backend claims.
- Project screenshots and portrait in `public/`.
- Existing resume: `public/cv/Nang_Dalet_CV.pdf`.
- Career and education content in `components/sections/about.tsx`.
- Existing contact details in `components/sections/contact.tsx`.
- Testimonials are not enabled on the homepage; do not manufacture endorsements or results.

## Product Principles

- Serve employers and clients equally.
- Let real project evidence substantiate capabilities.
- Keep projects, the CV, and contact accessible alongside the interactive experience.
- Preserve usability when motion or WebGL is unavailable.
